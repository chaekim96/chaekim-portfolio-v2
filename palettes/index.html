Sky blue and white, across the whole site

Option 1 now runs on every page of this test site: home, every case study, writing and reading. A glass bar at the bottom of each page sets how much blue and which accent. Your pick follows you from page to page, so browse around with it on.

How much blue

All three keep white type surfaces and navy text. Shown with hot pink.

Wash

The sky fades to a pale blue that carries down every page, instead of to white. Cards keep their own soft tints. The lightest touch.

Surfaces Suggested

A deeper sky, and every card, result box and design note turns a light blue. Blue is the whole site's material, while the work still sits on calm, even tiles.

Bold

The page itself is saturated sky blue, and the cards turn white so the work pops off it. The most blue, and the most poster-like.

Accents besides hot pink

One accent per site: the active tab, live dots, links on hover, section labels and the cursor glow. Shown on Surfaces.

Coral #ff6a3d Suggested

Sits almost opposite sky blue on the color wheel, so it's the strongest contrast. Warm, friendly, and still reads as playful like hot pink.
Sunflower #ffc21a

Sky and sun: cheerful and very bright. Buttons use dark text, and yellow text is darkened to stay readable.
Violet #7c5cff

A neighbor of blue, so it feels calm and techy rather than loud. Close to the AI Visibility Checker's lavender.
Cobalt #2563eb

All blue: a deep cobalt on sky. The most blue overall and the most serious, but the accent stands out least.
Mint #16c79a

Fresh and clean, like a pool. Pairs naturally with the green "live" dots.
Hot pink #ff2e8a

The original option 1, for comparison. Filled buttons use #e0006e so white text stays readable.

Try it

Glass: the tab bar, the case-page navigation, build labels, Try it live buttons and the switcher are liquid glass. In Chrome, Edge and Arc they bend the page behind them at the edges; Safari and Firefox show frosted glass. Every accent was checked so button text and accent-colored text meet the 4.5:1 contrast guideline. The live site isn't changed.
