Wash, with a green accent

Wash is the blue now: the sky fades to a pale blue that carries down every page. The accent is green, because green already means live on this site. The dots on "Try it live" and "Lately building" use it and gently pulse, so the work reads as running. The same green also colors the active tab, section labels and hover links.

Six greens

Open one to browse the whole site with it. The dots in the bar at the bottom of each page switch between them.

Signal green #30d158 Suggested

Apple-style green, the one most people read as "online". Bright and clear on the sky. Buttons use dark text.
Emerald #10b981

A touch deeper and cooler than signal green. Still lively, a little more grown-up.
Jade #0fa37f

Leans teal, so it sits closest to the blue and feels calm. Buttons use white text on a deeper jade.
Lime #9be22d

Yellow-green and very bright, the loudest of the set. Text in this green is darkened to stay readable.
Forest #15803d

Deep and serious. The live dots stand out least in brightness, but it reads as most trustworthy. White button text.
Sage #8fb996

Soft and muted, the quietest option. The live dot is the least eye-catching here.

Glass: the tab bar, case-page navigation, build labels, Try it live buttons and the switcher are liquid glass. In Chrome, Edge and Arc they bend the page behind them at the edges; Safari and Firefox show frosted glass. Button text and accent-colored text were checked against the 4.5:1 contrast guideline for each green. The dots pulse unless your system asks for reduced motion. The live site isn't changed.
