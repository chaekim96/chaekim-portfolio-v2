How the glass works

Each glass piece gets its own lens: a curved bezel computed with Snell's law bends the page behind it inward at the edges, with a slight color split, and a bright rim catches light from the top left. Built on kube.io's write-up on liquid glass refraction with CSS and SVG.

Browsers

Refraction runs in Chrome, Edge and Arc. Safari and Firefox get frosted glass with the same tint and rim. Reduced-motion settings soften the bend.

Readability

Glass is tinted enough that labels stay readable over any screenshot. On option 1, filled buttons use a deeper pink (#e0006e) so white text passes contrast; hot pink stays on dots and highlights.
