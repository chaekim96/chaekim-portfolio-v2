The mark: one tile, one recipe.

Every mark sits on the same tile. It is 64 units square with a corner radius of 14. One simple drawing goes on top. The drawing is ink (#111110) with one white detail.

The color comes from a base. The top of the tile is the base mixed 38% with white. The middle is the base. The bottom is the base mixed 10% with black. That is the whole recipe.

Worked example: Tally

top #ff857fbase #ff3b30bottom #e6352b
  • Base red #ff3b30.
  • The white slash holds 3.55 to 1 against the base. The bar is 1.8 to 1.
  • The ink bars hold 5.33 to 1. The bar is 3 to 1.

A script checks every mark against these numbers each time the site is built.

Small sizes: drawn by hand at 16 pixels.

Most people meet a mark as a browser tab icon, 16 pixels wide. A shrunk copy goes soft there. So each mark is also drawn pixel by pixel. Each pair shows the real size, then the same file enlarged.

Motion: only the white detail moves.

Four marks can move. Only the white detail animates, and only while you hover or focus the mark. They sit still otherwise, and always sit still if your device asks for less motion.

TallyThe slash draws across the bars.TypeRaceThe caret blinks.Token-FuelThe gauge arc fills and drains.Personal WikiThe center node pulses.

Line icons for small spaces.

A full color tile is too loud for a menu or a tab bar. So every glyph also has a line version on a 24 pixel grid, in two weights.
