07

Proofing and quality checklist

Before you approve the run

Digital proof

  • Every card has bleed and stays inside the safe zone
  • Card count and order match the BOM
  • Trait name and color on every question card match its category
  • [Subject] spelled the same on every card
  • Spelling check on all 54 questions and the rules

Physical sample

  • Colors match the approved proof
  • Front and back line up (no off-center backs)
  • Cards shuffle cleanly and do not stick
  • Deck fits the box without forcing
  • Shrink wrap tight, barcode scans
